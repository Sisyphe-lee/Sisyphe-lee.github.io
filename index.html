Chunyang Li   李春洋

I am a Ph.D. student at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Yiyi Liao. Before starting my Ph.D., I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Zhejiang University. I worked with Prof. Weiwei Xu at the CAD&CG Lab, Zhejiang University, and with Prof. Qixing Huang at The University of Texas at Austin.

Research Interests

  • Long-horizon agents. Building agents that can sustain multi-step software work through tool use, code editing, context and memory management, execution feedback, and experience summarization.
  • Physical agents. Developing agents that connect multimodal perception and generative world models with embodied decision-making, enabling robust reasoning and action in real physical environments.
