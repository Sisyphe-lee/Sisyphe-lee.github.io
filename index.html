Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Yiyi Liao. My research interests center on building Artificial General Intelligence through two directions: video generation models as world simulators, and tool-using agents that improve reasoning and real-world problem solving through interaction, execution feedback, and experience summarization.

Before starting my Ph.D., I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Zhejiang University. I have also worked as a research intern at the CAD&CG Lab, Zhejiang University, with Prof. Weiwei Xu, and as a summer research intern at The University of Texas at Austin with Prof. Qixing Huang.
